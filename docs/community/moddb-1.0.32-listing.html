World scale — blocks, square blocks, and miles (plain English)

In Vintage Story, 1 block ≈ 1 meter. A square block (block²) is one flat ground tile on the map floor — width 1 block × depth 1 block = 1 block².

The login overlay paints a circle on the ground around spawn. Below: radius (center to edge), end to end (if you walked straight across the circle), and square blocks inside the circle (total flat ground area).

Login bake disk today (1.0.32) — formula in code: 4.5 × 750 + 700 = 4,075 blocks radius

  • 4,075 blocks from spawn to the edge (radius) · 2.53 miles
  • 8,150 blocks end to end walking straight across the circle (diameter) · 5.06 miles
  • 52,168,110 square blocks (block²) of ground inside the circle · 20.1 square miles
  • Each bake grid cell is 64 × 64 = 4,096 block². About 12,868 cells fit in this circle. The overlay visits up to 1,680 scout stops inside it (not every cell).

  • 1.0.2: 72,000-block radius · 144,000 blocks end to end · 16,286,016,316 block² (44.7 mi radius · 89.5 mi across · 6,288 sq mi)
  • 1.0.3: 144,000-block radius · 288,000 blocks end to end · 65,144,065,265 block² (89.5 mi radius · 179 mi across · 25,152 sq mi)

1.0.29+ shrank the disk to the Farseer skyline band at login (4,075 blocks) because vanilla cannot stream land out to 36+ km; a thin ring and a frozen client were the result. The visit budget stayed 1,680 stops, not the full cell count.

What it does (1.0.32)

  • Real 3D far terrain from a persistent per-world cache; unlimited render cap with .dvfar 0
  • Join overlay (singleplayer + Distant Vistas servers): 16 parallel scouts bake the 4,075-block / 8,150-block-across disk while you stay at spawn; graphics view held at 750 blocks then restored
  • Vanilla public servers: overlay skipped — no teleports; land fills as you walk
  • Live seasonal colour and frost on far vegetation; visited land stays drawn behind the camera
  • Farseer in the background; Distant Vistas tiles on top; gray tent + black tips at the rim (SkyTint ~0.4 in Ctrl+Shift+F)
  • ChunkLOD / TopoHorizon defer unless .dvdefer off

People ask: what is the difference from Vintage Horizons?

Same starting idea — LOD cache from client chunk data. Different draw path:

  • No parent plates — Horizons uses coarse parent boxes as stand-ins (giant square shelves). Distant Vistas waits for real land.
  • Trail behind camera — captured L0/L1 stays drawn when you fly away; Horizons drops tiles to sky.
  • Vanilla handoff — 3D + look-down, not a flat disc; patched chunk shaders (full alpha at view ring, no fog slice).
  • Night + season — live ambient multiply; live season shader clock on vegetation only.
  • Farseer — draws with Farseer behind; shader overlay without Harmony patches.
  • Login — you stay at spawn; scouts bake cells (1.0.31+: 16 parallel). Horizons hop-teleported the player.
  • Ocean — sealed seabed under water (0.7.11 cake-slice fix). Capture — per-quadrant skip fix (0.7.7 checkerboard).

Install

Drop distantvistas_1.0.32.zip in Mods. Do not extract. Fully quit Vintage Story, then start again.

Recommended: Client MeshRef Leak Fix (vsvaogc) — client only.

Commands

  • .dvistas — status, meshes, far edge
  • .dvdetail [blocks] — detail halving distance (default 512; full 1-block detail out to ~2× this in blocks)
  • .dvfar <blocks> — cap LOD distance (0 = unlimited; max 262,144 blocks)
  • .dvdefer [on|off] — defer for ChunkLOD / TopoHorizon (restart to apply)
