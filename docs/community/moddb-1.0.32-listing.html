Public servers

From 1.0.1 onward: vanilla public servers that do not have Distant Vistas skip the join overlay on purpose. The old 1.0 hop-scan teleported you and sent a gamemode command, which kicked people off those servers. Overlay, hops, /gamemode, client teleports, and the 750 view hold are skipped when the assist channel is not connected. Capture while you walk, same as 0.7.78. Singleplayer and servers that also run Distant Vistas still get the overlay.

Official 1.0.32

This is official Distant Vistas 1.0.32. It took a long process to get here. A lot of work went into far land, seasons, frost, Farseer coexistence, and making the join overlay faster without moving your character around the map.

Hey: there is still a loading screen when you join singleplayer (or a Distant Vistas server). It should be faster than 1.0.1 — sixteen scouts work in parallel and 1.0.32 cuts a lot of wasted work during colour sampling — but it is not instant. You might still wait several minutes on a big world. Seasons are involved. The overlay paints a disk of far land, samples live colour, and bakes what you will see on the horizon so summer, winter, frost, and snow match the ground when you spawn. On a vanilla public server that pass is skipped on purpose.

New since 1.0.1: you stay at your pickup spot while scouts stream and bake around visit cells — no hop-scan teleport wedge. The overlay should show 16/16 scouts within seconds and the percent bar should move every few seconds. When it ends you should be at the same X/Y/Z and look direction you had when it started. Far coverage reaches the Farseer silhouette band (~4075 blocks at the 750 graphics hold). Farseer meets our land as gray tent smoke with black mountain tips — not a white sky strip at the join (1.0.32).

What it does

Far terrain past vanilla view distance. A persistent level-of-detail cache from chunks your client already has. Real 3D land, trees, water, and builds you already saw. Not a fog silhouette.

  • Join overlay. Four-season Distant Vistas splash. Scouts paint a disk of far land so hills exist when you spawn — you stay at spawn; they do not hop your character cell to cell. Sixteen parallel scouts (1.0.31+). Graphics view is held at 750 for that pass, then your real slider comes back. Overlay keeps Windows moving. Mouse look and world audio restore when it ends. Esc goes to the menu. Overlay is singleplayer and Distant Vistas servers only. Vanilla public servers skip it.
  • Seasons. Overlay and walking share one bake from the live month. Summer is bright green. Winter without a snow sheet is mottled brown, tan, and olive. Real snow stays snow. Spring and autumn follow the same calendar mix. Autumn trees and bushes keep species colour on far LOD. /time while you are already in the world does not retint. Change month, then rejoin. After 30 days, or a later calendar month, the overlay recaptures.
  • December frost. Far pines and oaks frost: green or orange sides, frost-white tops. Frosted grass keeps live colour.
  • Visited land stays. Land you already walked stays on the horizon when you leave.
  • Vanilla join. LOD only steps aside when that tile is actually loaded. Looking down still draws far land where vanilla has no chunks. Captured land stays behind and beside the camera. Incomplete far tiles wait until they are real land.
  • Night and the view-distance ring. Night uses the same live ambient as vanilla chunks. LOD joins vanilla at the view-distance ring at full strength.
  • Clouds. Cloud tiles stretch to the LOD horizon so you do not get a nearby cloud box over far mountains.
  • Farseer, ChunkLOD, TopoHorizon. Farseer sits in the background. Distant Vistas tiles sit on top where we have them. Gray tent smoke with black mountain tips at the far rim (keep Farseer SkyTint around 0.4 in Ctrl+Shift+F). We overlay their region shaders without Harmony patches. ChunkLOD and TopoHorizon still make us sit out unless you set .dvdefer off.
  • Pause-on-Start. If you use Pause-on-Start, the overlay still ticks while the splash is up; when it finishes or you Esc out, pause and the menu come back.

Drop distantvistas_1.0.32.zip in Mods. Do not extract. Fully quit Vintage Story, then start it again. Leaving to the menu does not load a new zip.

After you join, check: 16/16 scouts and percent moving; same spawn spot when overlay ends; no holes underfoot; far land meets gray/black Farseer silhouette without a white gap; FPS back.

What works for me may not work for you. Different worlds, hardware, and servers behave differently. If Horizons or another LOD mod already does what you need, use that.

Recommended: Client MeshRef Leak Fix

I run this with Client MeshRef Leak Fix (vsvaogc). It is client-only. A dedicated server does not need it. Distant Vistas does not require it to load.

Singleplayer vs client-side

  • Singleplayer: install on the client. This is what I built it for and what I test.
  • Client-side: it draws from what the client already loaded. You do not need a server install for that. That is the point. On vanilla public servers the overlay is skipped so hop-scan cannot kick you.
  • Server assist: exists if you put it on a server too. That is not the first thing I built this for.

In-game commands

  • .dvistas — status, meshes, far edge, server assist
  • .dvdetail [blocks] — detail halving distance (default 512)
  • .dvfar <blocks> — cap LOD distance (0 = unlimited)
  • .dvdefer [on|off] — defer when ChunkLOD or TopoHorizon draws (restart to apply)
